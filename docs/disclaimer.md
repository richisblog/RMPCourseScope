# Project and data notice

CourseScope is an independent information lookup tool maintained by Richisblog. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the University of California, Davis (UC Davis), Rate My Professors (RMP), or any AI provider.

Professor ratings and review text originate from student-submitted opinions on external services. They may be incomplete, outdated, biased, or inaccurate. Course links may be inferred from labels and review context; confidence scores describe the strength of a matching rule, not certainty that a student took a course. Aggregated statistics describe the stored review sample and are not official grades, enrollment counts, teaching evaluations, or representative estimates for all students.

Official course titles, descriptions, units, prerequisites, and General Education categories are sourced from the UC Davis General Catalog. Catalog data can change; use the linked official catalog page for current information. Always verify course availability, instruction mode, dates, and requirements with UC Davis before making academic decisions.

CourseScope is provided for convenient searching and research. It does not make academic decisions, provide university advising, or warrant that external source data is complete or current. Third-party names and marks are used only to identify sources and belong to their respective owners.
